Right where you left off.

Continue your current book from Home. Filter your shelves without losing your place, browse ebook authors and reopen recent reads from your history.

A few books for the road.

Download books or whole series, follow transfers and read offline. Optionally remove a series’ finished downloads after at least 24 hours. Progress syncs to Komga when you reconnect.

Make the page comfortable.

Choose reading direction, page layout and gestures, with reading defaults for each visible library. Adjust ebook typography, add bookmarks and open book or series details from the reader.

Your shelves, your way.

Keep several Komga connections, edit each account and choose its visible libraries. Explore series details, author pages and catalogue links, then select volumes from a floating toolbar.

One choice for every image.

Set image scaling and colour filters for all image formats together. Adjust sharpening on the same page, and manage preloading separately.

A comfortable space to read.

Choose from twelve light and dark palettes, including Mint and Lavender. Follow the system theme, adjust covers for each orientation and restore a settings page to its defaults.
