Right where you left off.

Your shelves, your way.

Keep several Komga connections and choose the visible libraries for each account. Combine catalogue filters, save searches and browse series, author pages and publication links.

A few books for the road.

Download books or whole series, follow transfers and read offline. For selected series, keep the next books you have yet to finish available offline. You can also remove a book’s download at least 24 hours after finishing it. Progress syncs to Komga when you reconnect.

Make the page comfortable.

Set reading defaults for each library with clear categories and dropdown choices. Adjust page layout, ebook typography and gestures, add bookmarks and open book or series details from the reader.

One choice for every image.

Image scaling, colour filters, sharpening and preloading share one page, grouped by category.

A comfortable space to read.

Choose from twelve light and dark palettes, follow the system theme and adjust covers for each orientation. Choose which metadata appears on series and author pages; tags and language are hidden by default.
